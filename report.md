## Five recommendations

1. **[Sinners](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/sinners_2025)** (2025; 97% / 96%; on HBO Max): Ryan Coogler's Jim Crow-era vampire epic, with Michael B. Jordan playing twin brothers. It's a blues musical, a gangster film and a horror movie at once, and it's one of the few 2025 films that both critics and audiences loved (97/96). For the adults only: rated R and gory.
2. **[Project Hail Mary](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/project_hail_mary)** (2026; 95% / 95%; on MGM+, Amazon Prime Video): Ryan Gosling as a science teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship and has to save Earth. It's a funny, warm, problem-solving space adventure built around a lovable alien friendship (95/95). PG-13, so it works for older kids but is a bit intense for a 4-year-old.
3. **[Hoppers](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/hoppers)** (2026; 94% / 93%; on Disney+): Pixar's original comedy about a girl who 'hops' her mind into a robotic beaver to talk to animals. It's fresh, very funny, and has an eco-minded heart (94/93). Rated PG, and the best pick here for a 4- and 6-year-old.
4. **[The Ballad of Wallis Island](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_ballad_of_wallis_island)** (2025; 97% / 93%; on Peacock): A gentle, very funny British comedy. An eccentric lottery winner pays his favorite folk duo to reunite on his remote island. It's sweet, with lovely original songs and no cynicism (97/93). PG-13, but mild: easy viewing for a parents' night. Watch it soon: JustWatch says it leaves Peacock on Dec 6, 2026.
5. **[KPop Demon Hunters](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/kpop_demon_hunters)** (2025; 92% / 99%; on Netflix): A K-pop girl group who secretly fight demons. It has kinetic animation and genuinely catchy songs, and its Popcornmeter (99) is tied for the highest of any qualifier, with a Tomatometer of 92. Rated PG; the 6-year-old will probably love it, and the 4-year-old may find the demons a little scary.
