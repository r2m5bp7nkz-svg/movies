Five recommendations

  1. Sinners (2025; 97% / 96%; on HBO Max): Ryan Coogler's Jim Crow-era vampire epic, with Michael B. Jordan playing twin brothers. It's a blues musical, a gangster film and a horror movie at once, and it's one of the few 2025 films that both critics and audiences loved (97/96). For the adults only: rated R and gory.
  2. Project Hail Mary (2026; 95% / 95%; on MGM+, Amazon Prime Video): Ryan Gosling as a science teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship and has to save Earth. It's a funny, warm, problem-solving space adventure built around a lovable alien friendship (95/95). PG-13, so it works for older kids but is a bit intense for a 4-year-old.
  3. Hoppers (2026; 94% / 93%; on Disney+): Pixar's original comedy about a girl who 'hops' her mind into a robotic beaver to talk to animals. It's fresh, very funny, and has an eco-minded heart (94/93). Rated PG, and the best pick here for a 4- and 6-year-old.
  4. The Ballad of Wallis Island (2025; 97% / 93%; on Peacock): A gentle, very funny British comedy. An eccentric lottery winner pays his favorite folk duo to reunite on his remote island. It's sweet, with lovely original songs and no cynicism (97/93). PG-13, but mild: easy viewing for a parents' night. Watch it soon: JustWatch says it leaves Peacock on Dec 6, 2026.
  5. KPop Demon Hunters (2025; 92% / 99%; on Netflix): A K-pop girl group who secretly fight demons. It has kinetic animation and genuinely catchy songs, and its Popcornmeter (99) is tied for the highest of any qualifier, with a Tomatometer of 92. Rated PG; the 6-year-old will probably love it, and the 4-year-old may find the demons a little scary.

Method and coverage

  • Discovery: the full JustWatch US catalog of 2025–2026 movies for each service's own subscription package (3176 distinct titles), plus Rotten Tomatoes' "new fresh movies on <service>" lists, each looked up again on JustWatch. RT has no Starz or MGM+ list.
  • Scores: 2568 RT movie pages were fetched and parsed today, using the scorecard JSON (Tomatometer, critic review count, Popcornmeter, rating band). If RT shows no Popcornmeter, the table shows "—".
  • Year rule: the earliest of the IMDb year and the RT year, i.e. the first release, festivals included. Streaming dates are never used. Disagreements are flagged ʸ.
  • Not films: IMDb types short, TV short, TV special (stand-up, concerts, live broadcasts), episodes and series are excluded, as is anything 40 minutes or shorter.
  • Availability: JustWatch FLATRATE on the service's own package, confirmed on the service's own title page. Prime pages were read through Amazon's entitlement message, and "Subscribe to <channel>", "rent or buy" and "Watch for free" (ads) were all rejected. Peacock was checked through its public watch-online page. Reelgood and RT where-to-watch are cross-checks. 304 source URLs were logged.
  • Excluded despite ≥80%: 61 × first released 2024 (before 2025); 9 × first released 2023 (before 2025); 6 × TV special (stand-up/concert/live broadcast), not a film; 1 × stand-up/comedy special (RT genre 'Stand-Up'), not a film; 1 × short film (IMDb type tvShort); 1 × short film (IMDb type short); 1 × first released 2022 (before 2025); 1 × first released 2015 (before 2025); 1 × Hulu's own listing files it under /series/ (59 min); not clearly a feature film.
  • Service-page rejections among ≥80% candidates: disney: 1 bundle; disney: 48 unreachable; hulu: 1 unreachable; netflix: 1 unreachable; peacock: 1 not_listed; prime: 3 channel_addon; prime: 4 free_ads; prime: 2 rent. "Unreachable" for Disney+ means RT listed "Disney+" but JustWatch had no Disney+-own listing (these are Hulu titles that appear in Disney+ through the bundle).
