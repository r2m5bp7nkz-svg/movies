How to read this: A movie makes a service's main table only if its Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer is at least 80%, its Popcornmeter is at least 80% (and RT actually displays one), and it has at least 20 critic reviews. Tables are ranked by Tomatometer, ties broken by Popcornmeter, then by critic review count. Movies that clear 80% with critics but have fewer than 20 reviews, or that have no Popcornmeter yet, are listed separately under each table and are not ranked in it. IMDb ratings (out of 10, vote count in parentheses) are for information only and are never used as a filter. Both RT scores were read from each movie's own RT page today; where the Popcornmeter is missing, RT's page really shows none (checked in the scorecard markup, the embedded JSON and the audience-reviews page). Availability required two things: JustWatch lists the movie as included with the subscription (FLATRATE on the service's own package) and the service's own public title page confirms it. Reelgood and RT's "where to watch" were used as further cross-checks. Rentals, purchases, Amazon Channels, free-with-ads, and Hulu-in-Disney+ bundle access are excluded.

Five recommendations

  1. Sinners (2025; 97% / 96%; IMDb 7.5 (535,509); on HBO Max): Ryan Coogler's Jim Crow-era vampire epic, with Michael B. Jordan playing twin brothers. It's a blues musical, a gangster film and a horror movie at once, and it's one of the few 2025 films that both critics and audiences loved (97/96). For the adults only: rated R and gory.
  2. Project Hail Mary (2026; 95% / 95%; IMDb 8.2 (584,479); on MGM+, Amazon Prime Video): Ryan Gosling as a science teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship and has to save Earth. It's a funny, warm, problem-solving space adventure built around a lovable alien friendship (95/95). PG-13, so it works for older kids but is a bit intense for a 4-year-old.
  3. Hoppers (2026; 94% / 93%; IMDb 7.2 (89,063); on Disney+): Pixar's original comedy about a girl who 'hops' her mind into a robotic beaver to talk to animals. It's fresh, very funny, and has an eco-minded heart (94/93). Rated PG, and the best pick here for a 4- and 6-year-old.
  4. The Sheep Detectives (2026; 95% / 96%; IMDb 7.4 (123,038); on Amazon Prime Video): A family comedy and murder mystery in which a flock of sheep set out to solve their shepherd's death. It's clever, warm and silly at once, and both critics and audiences love it (95/96, from 218 reviews). Rated PG: the 6-year-old should love it, and it's gentle enough to try with the 4-year-old. Included with Prime Video. Alternate for the adults: The Ballad of Wallis Island (Peacock, 97/93; leaves Dec 6).
  5. KPop Demon Hunters (2025; 92% / 99%; IMDb 7.4 (144,936); on Netflix): A K-pop girl group who secretly fight demons. It has kinetic animation and genuinely catchy songs, and its Popcornmeter (99) is tied for the highest of any qualifier, with a Tomatometer of 92. Rated PG; the 6-year-old will probably love it, and the 4-year-old may find the demons a little scary.

Method and coverage

  • Discovery: the full JustWatch US catalog of 2025–2026 movies for each service's own subscription package (3176 distinct titles), plus Rotten Tomatoes' "new fresh movies on <service>" lists, each looked up again on JustWatch. RT has no Starz or MGM+ list.
  • Scores: 2568 RT movie pages were fetched and parsed today, using the scorecard JSON (Tomatometer, critic review count, Popcornmeter, rating band). If RT shows no Popcornmeter, the table shows "—". Every such movie was re-checked by audience_audit.py (scorecard markup, embedded JSON, trailing-slash variant, audience-reviews page, RT API probes) and the evidence is stored.
  • IMDb: user rating and vote count from IMDb's official daily ratings dataset (title.ratings.tsv.gz), which carries the same numbers as each title page. The pages themselves show scripted clients a human-verification wall. Informational only.
  • Year rule: the earliest of the IMDb year and the RT year, i.e. the first release, festivals included. Streaming dates are never used. Disagreements are flagged ʸ.
  • Not films: IMDb types short, TV short, TV special (stand-up, concerts, live broadcasts), episodes and series are excluded, as is anything 40 minutes or shorter.
  • Availability: JustWatch FLATRATE on the service's own package, confirmed on the service's own title page. Prime pages were read through Amazon's entitlement message, and "Subscribe to <channel>", "rent or buy" and "Watch for free" (ads) were all rejected. Peacock was checked through its public watch-online page. Reelgood and RT where-to-watch are cross-checks. 646 source URLs were logged.
  • Excluded despite ≥80%: 61 × first released 2024 (before 2025); 9 × first released 2023 (before 2025); 6 × TV special (stand-up/concert/live broadcast), not a film; 1 × stand-up/comedy special (RT genre 'Stand-Up'), not a film; 1 × short film (IMDb type tvShort); 1 × short film (IMDb type short); 1 × first released 2022 (before 2025); 1 × first released 2015 (before 2025); 1 × Hulu's own listing files it under /series/ (59 min); not clearly a feature film.
  • Service-page rejections among ≥80% candidates: disney: 1 bundle; disney: 48 unreachable; hulu: 1 unreachable; netflix: 1 unreachable; peacock: 1 not_listed; prime: 3 channel_addon; prime: 4 free_ads; prime: 2 rent. "Unreachable" for Disney+ means RT listed "Disney+" but JustWatch had no Disney+-own listing (these are Hulu titles that appear in Disney+ through the bundle).
